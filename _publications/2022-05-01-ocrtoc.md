We describe our approach to the **ICRA 2022 Open Cloud Robot Table Organization Challenge (OCRTOC)**, where we achieved **3rd place globally**. Our system integrates robotic perception, task planning, and manipulation to rearrange objects on a tabletop from an initial configuration to a goal configuration.
