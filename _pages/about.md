I am an MS Robotics student (thesis) at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://www.cmu.edu/), working with [Prof. Deepak Pathak](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~dpathak/) on robot learning and physics engines.

Before CMU, I was a research assistant at [IIIT-Hyderabad](https://www.iiit.ac.in/), spending a few years thinking carefully about how robots move through uncertain environments. That work spanned motion planning with diffusion models, robotic manipulation, and tabletop perception, advised by [Prof. K. Madhava Krishna](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QDuPGHwAAAAJ), [Prof. Arun Kumar Singh](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=P2h0OVAAAAAJ), [Prof. Makarand Tapaswi](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=GFnEEloAAAAJ), and [Dr. Vladimir Petrik](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=L5l5jqEAAAAJ). It led to papers at ICRA 2024 and CASE 2023, and a 3rd-place finish at the ICRA Open Cloud Robot Table Organization Challenge in 2022. I completed my B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering from IIIT-H.

The question I keep coming back to: what is it going to take before robots are capable enough to go do science for us?
